About Us – Znow

At Znow, we believe great coffee should always be just minutes away.

Znow is building India’s coffee-focused quick-commerce platform, bringing café-style coffee to your doorstep in minutes. We combine technology, convenience, and quality to make your daily coffee ritual faster, easier, and more enjoyable.

From rich espressos and creamy lattes to refreshing iced coffees, Znow brings your favourite coffee experiences closer to you through our Cloud Cafe model and technology-driven delivery network.

With our Daily feature, your coffee routine becomes effortless. Simply select your favourite coffee, choose your preferred delivery time, set the duration of your plan, pick your start date, and make the payment. That’s it! Your chosen coffee will be delivered to your doorstep at the same time every day for your selected duration, so you never have to place the same order again and again.

Whether you need your morning caffeine fix, a boost during work, or a coffee break between classes, Znow makes it easier to enjoy your coffee exactly when you want it.
